# ASSESSMENT BRIEF

## MRP5025 Data Science Capstone Project 2

## Assessment 2: Presentation of the data science product

### Overview

**Task type:** Presentation

**Method:** Individual

**Weighting:** 30%

**Due date:** 23:59 MYT Tuesday, Week 7

**Length/time:** 20 minutes (maximum)

**Subject learning outcome:**

- SLO3: Defend ideas and research outcomes with all stakeholders in an ethical and professional manner (A5).

### Instructions

1. Prepare a video presentation on the Data Science product with statement of problem statement, objectives, literature review, methodology, solution and results.
2. Ensure that you are visible throughout the video presentation.
3. The following are some points for your consideration:
    - Plan your presentation. What are the key points you want to make? What supporting evidence do you have? What visuals will you use? Create an outline to help you organize your thoughts and ensure that your presentation is focused and well-paced.
    - Choose a video recording platform. There are many different video recording platforms available, such as PowerPoint, Screencast-o-matic and Zoom. Choose one that is easy to use and has the features you need, such as screen sharing and the ability to record high-quality video and audio.
    - Record your presentation. Make sure to record in a quiet and well-lit room. Speak clearly and slowly, and use gestures to engage your audience. If you are using visuals, make sure they are clear and easy to see.
    - Edit your presentation. Once you have recorded your presentation, you may want to edit it to remove any mistakes or unwanted footage. You may also want to add music, transitions, or other effects.
4. Upload your video (.mp4) to your iMail OneDrive. Copy and paste the shared link to access the video on a page after the Cover Page. In the sharing link settings, ensure that the link works for anyone.
5. Structure your presentation as follows:
    - Summarise your project background, objectives and literature gap analysis to formulate the methodology
    - Justify your selection of the development tools/instruments
    - Present and interpret your project findings and results
    - Discuss and conclude how your project incorporated data science theories and contributed to new knowledge/concept/theory.
