Step 4 — GLM: Poisson vs Negative Binomial
============================================================

Script  : src/4_model_glm.py
Outputs : data/2years/4_irr_table.csv, 4_model_comparison.csv, 4_residuals.png

Formula : stressed ~ week_number + C(day_of_week_name, Treatment('Monday'))

Overdispersion: Mean=100.4, Variance=748.97, Ratio=7.33 -> NB confirmed

Model comparison:
  Poisson           AIC = 8904.97
  Negative Binomial AIC = 6523.46  <- Winner (delta AIC = 2381)

IRR table (NB model):
  Intercept (Mon, wk 0)   97.9   ***
  Tuesday                  1.023  ns
  Wednesday                1.022  ns
  Thursday                 0.977  ns
  Friday                   0.893  **   (-11% vs Monday)
  Saturday                 0.817  ***  (-18% vs Monday)
  Sunday                   0.853  ***  (-15% vs Monday)
  week_number              1.003  ***  (+0.3%/week, +37% over 2 years)
  alpha                    1.053  ***

Key findings:
- Mon-Thu not significantly different
- Weekend effect confirmed: Sat worst (-18%), then Sun (-15%), Fri (-11%)
- Upward trend: +37% stressed posts over 2 years
- NB justified by overdispersion ratio 7.33 and significant alpha

Step 5 — Time Series Forecasting: Prophet vs SARIMA
============================================================

Script  : src/5_forecast.py
Outputs : data/2years/5_cv_scores.csv, 5_cv_summary.csv, 5_cv_plot.png,
          5_final_forecast.csv, 5_final_forecast.png

Models:
- Prophet: yearly + weekly seasonality, multiplicative mode
- SARIMA(1,1,1)(1,1,1,7): weekly period=7, replaces flat ARIMA(1,1,1)

Validation: walk-forward expanding, 4 windows, horizon=21d, min_train=180d

CV results:
  Window  Train days  Prophet MAE  SARIMA MAE
  1       180         77.6         18.6
  2       348         20.9         18.3
  3       516         18.8         12.9
  4       684         10.7         12.5   <- Prophet wins this window
  Mean                32.01        15.57  <- SARIMA wins overall

Key findings:
- SARIMA wins overall (MAE 15.57 vs 32.01)
- Prophet needs 1 full year to learn yearly seasonality (Window 1 MAE=77.6)
- Prophet beats SARIMA on Window 4 with full data (MAE 10.7 vs 12.5)
- Final forecast: declining trend Dec 2025, recovery from Jan 2026 (~36-68/day)
  Note: forecast is lower than prior year actuals (~63-142/day) — limitation of
  having only one prior December in training data

CLI: python src/5_forecast.py --input data/2years/3_daily_counts.csv
     --output-dir data/2years --horizon 21 --windows 4
     --min-train 180 --window-type [expanding|sliding]

Fixes:
- SARIMA reindexes to full daily range before fitting (gap safety)
- Added --window-type arg (expanding recommended; sliding worse for Prophet)
